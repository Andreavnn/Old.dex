SOURCE / DATA FINDING
Old.dex uses two complementary sources. Old World Builder data supplies roster structure, selectable options, points, and nested choices. tow.whfb.app supplies the live rules-reference layer used to enrich model profiles, weapon profiles, rule links, and rule text.

For Grand Cannon, the current transport-readable tow.whfb.app weapon page contains Range 48, Strength 10 and AP -3, but its Special Rules table cell is delivered as an empty/dash value while Notes still expose Cannon Fire and the Black Powder Misfire table. The user's browser-rendered page shows the complete six-rule Special Rules list. This difference explains why the old enrichment path could not recover all six rules from a normal HTTP/reader fetch and why it incorrectly promoted the misfire-table Notes link.

V0.61 RESPONSE
The weapon parser now treats the canonical Weapons of War page as authoritative, retries the live page once without cache when its rule cell is incomplete, parses rule links/text separately from Notes, removes weapon-name pseudo-rules, and does not turn reference-table links into Special Rules. A canonical weapon-page fallback mechanism covers confirmed transport/rendering gaps; Grand Cannon is seeded with the browser-visible six-rule set, and the fallback is bypassed automatically whenever the live source returns explicit rules itself.
