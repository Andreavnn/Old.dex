SOURCE-RULE DATA HANDLING
The v0.61 weapon pipeline already treats canonical Weapons of War pages as the authority and retries incomplete live pages before using a narrowly scoped canonical weapon-page supplement. v0.62 applies the same defensive principle to Special Rules: source metadata and flavour are not accepted as mechanics merely because the transport flattened them into one text block. The extractor now scores sentence-sized candidates and the UI fallback requires mechanical language. This is a general source-quality guard rather than a Fear of Elves exception.

MATCH SETUP SCOPE
This build intentionally starts the guided match assistant with Setup and Overview. Tips for later battle phases/subphases remain future work. Wizard spell recording is a match-state aid: the user still resolves random spell generation according to the rules and records the resulting spell set. Priests expose their available prayers without forcing a pre-game selection when the source rules do not require one.
