Old.dex v0.66 direct-source update

Upload the CONTENTS of this package to the root of Andreavnn/Old.dex on main,
replacing matching paths and adding new files/directories.

Do not upload it as a nested Old.dex-v0.66-DIRECT-SOURCE folder.

After deployment, perform a hard refresh (or fully close/reopen the installed PWA once)
so the v0.66 service-worker cache is active.

Primary v0.66 changes
---------------------
- Standardized click/input behavior and removed stray text carets from non-editable UI.
- Magical Item selection now uses explicit shared checkbox/label/details controls.
- Strategy through End of Round supports Your Turn / Enemy's Turn filtering.
- Friendly phase/subphase rules are loaded from canonical rule pages, cached, and filtered by turn context.
- End of Round uses Back / Your Turn / Enemy's Turn / End of Round controls.
- Only End of Round increments roundsCompleted and advances the match round.
